<threat_model>

- Untrusted source is never executed; Clang runs without a shell.
- Input is capped at 512 KiB and compilation at 30 seconds.
- AST output is written inside a temporary directory and rejected above 64 MiB before parsing.
- Residual risk: Clang may fill temporary disk space during the timeout window; the short deadline, small source limit, isolated directory, and cleanup reduce but do not eliminate it.

</threat_model>
